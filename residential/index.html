Upfront Pricing

Straight Answers and Upfront Prices

No homeowner likes surprises on the final bill. So Wausau HVAC quotes every Wausau job before the work begins, after a full diagnostic that looks at the equipment's age, fuel type, and tonnage, refrigerant levels, coil and heat exchanger condition, and the ductwork feeding each room. You'll know the problem, what the fix involves, and exactly what it will cost before you say yes.

Many HVAC problems stay hidden until a failure is well underway. Refrigerant seeps out through pinhole leaks in the evaporator coil, blower bearings wear slowly, and a weakening capacitor gives no warning until the compressor won't start on the first hot day. If you notice weak airflow, rising energy bills, ice on the refrigerant lines, or rooms that never reach the thermostat setting, schedule an inspection right away. Catching it early usually keeps a repairable issue from becoming a replacement.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most air conditioners and furnaces last 12 to 18 years, and a planned replacement is always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap during a heat wave or cold snap. Our technicians know the climate demands common to Wausau and how they affect equipment life, so we can tell you honestly whether your system has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

The best repair is the one you never need. That's the idea behind our seasonal plans. Twice a year, we clean coils and burners, confirm the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all matched to the equipment in your home.

Most homes do best with two visits a year: a springtime tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating. We adjust the schedule to the age of your equipment, how hard your household runs it, and local conditions like dust and pollen, which coat condenser coils and raise energy use fast.

Think of maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most families, a plan costs far less than replacing the system ahead of schedule.

Sized for Your Home

The Right Size System for Your Home

Getting the size right matters more than most homeowners realize. Before quoting new equipment, Wausau HVAC works through the details that determine your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. That gives you a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, controls humidity, and keeps every room at the temperature you set.

Picking a new system comes down to more than a SEER2 number. We'll compare heat pumps against a furnace-and-AC pair, and ducted against ductless, walking through operating costs, electrical and venting work, and the installation itself. Families with a growing household frequently do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds more even temperatures across the whole house and uses less energy. You choose what fits your home and budget.

Common Problems

The HVAC Repairs Wausau Homes Need Most

The majority of repair calls we get in Wausau come down to a handful of recurring problems. The AC runs but the air never gets cold. The furnace won't light, or it lights and shuts off minutes later. Ice builds up on the evaporator coil, the condenser hums without starting, or a mini-split starts flashing error codes. Refrigerant leaks, clogged condensate drains, and thermostats that forget their settings round out the list. Our technicians handle all of them, always targeting the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A bad capacitor just keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak is fixed.
