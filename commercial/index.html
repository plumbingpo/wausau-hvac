Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Wausau HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan visits around your hours.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safety

Professional Installation and Documentation

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.
